
# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want CodeCabinate to be a respectful and constructive environment for
everyone who participates in the project.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors and participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Communicate constructively.
- Accept reasonable criticism.
- Focus on technical issues rather than individuals.
- Respect different opinions and perspectives.
- Provide useful and constructive feedback.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment.
- Personal attacks.
- Threats.
- Discrimination.
- Hate speech.
- Intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Malicious or abusive behavior.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close Issues, reject Pull Requests,
or restrict participation when necessary to maintain a respectful project
environment.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the project
maintainers through the project's official communication channels.

For security vulnerabilities, please follow `SECURITY.md` instead.
